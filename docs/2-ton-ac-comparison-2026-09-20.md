# 2-ton wall-mounted inverter AC comparison: Japan Electronics vs Umar Electronics vs Friends Electronics

Data pulled 2026-09-20 02:30 to 03:30 PKT from japanelectronics.com.pk (Shopify product feed, 65-item 2 Ton collection plus a sweep of all 310 AC listings), umarelectronics.pk (WooCommerce Store API, 50-item 2 Ton category plus all 260 AC listings), friendselectronics.com (Friends Corporation, Rawalpindi/Islamabad, 181-item AC category), kenwoodpakistan.pk (official catalog), samsung.com/pk, lg.com, haier.com/pk, and Pakistani forum/review sites. "n/s" = not stated on that store's page. Floor-standing, cassette and ceiling units are excluded from the table.

## Bottom line

- The shop's "KES 24522 at Rs 300,000" is the KES-2452FHI Superia Smart, a floor-standing cabinet. Japan Electronics lists it at exactly Rs 300,000 (out of stock); Kenwood's own site lists it at Rs 365,000 (out of stock); Umar has it price-on-call. It is excluded on requirement 1.
- The shop's "KES 24625 at Rs 200,000" is the KES-2462S eSupreme Pro wall split. Umar has it in stock at Rs 220,000. It has no WiFi and no T3 rating on any listing, is R32 with gold fins (no copper claim), and no longer appears in Kenwood's current official catalog. It fails requirements 2 and 3.
- No 2-ton wall unit at either store publishes an EER of 4.0. The best published EERs with WiFi and T3 are 3.6 (Midea Xtreme, Midea Breezeless, Kenwood eLuxury Pro), then 3.5 (TCL T5 Smart), then 3.32 (TCL T3 Pro). Kenwood's official catalog has an EER 4.0 2-ton wall model (E-Smart Onyx KES-2466S, WiFi, T3, 60C) but neither store sells it and Kenwood shows it out of stock.
- Nobody, owner or reviewer, confirmed a copper condenser on any shortlisted model. Copper is a marketing claim only (Midea "100% copper pipe", Orient/Panasonic "100% copper", PEL "copper coils", Gree AITH24S and Acson "copper condenser"). Inspect the outdoor coil before paying.
- Friends Electronics: the Rawalpindi/Islamabad retailer is Friends Corporation (five outlets: Blue Area, Centaurus, Faizabad Murree Road and others, phone +92 304 8444499) and its working web store is friendselectronics.com. It lists 29 two-ton wall inverters with prices and stock but every product page says "Product information will be available soon", so no WiFi, EER, noise or warranty data exists there. It does not stock either Kenwood model. friendselectronics.pk is an unrelated, half-configured Karachi shop and was not used for the table. No Facebook or Instagram listings were retrievable (login wall).
- Noise: only 10 of 106 listings publish indoor dB. Quietest published: Samsung 44/28, LG 46/42/37/28 (Pakistan dealer sheet) or 56 to 32 (LG UAE sheet), Bosch 48.5/41/34, Haier HFPCA 47/43/39, TCL T3 Pro and T5 47 (high only), Midea Xtreme 50/45/42 (dealer). Haier HSU-24HFCD is loud at 62.

## 4. Where websites contradict the shop quotes and the "known findings"
