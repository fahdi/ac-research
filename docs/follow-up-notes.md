## 1.5 ton or 2 ton for a 7 x 4 m hall

2 ton. Rough peak load for 28 m2 with a 3 m ceiling at 45C outside and 25C inside is about 6 to 6.5 kW (20,000 to 22,000 BTU/h) including 4 to 6 people and appliances. A 1.5 ton delivers roughly 15,000 to 16,000 BTU/h after derating at 45C, so it runs flat out on June afternoons and never reaches set point. A 2 ton delivers roughly 20,000 to 21,000 BTU/h derated and cruises at 60 to 80 percent load, where an inverter is most efficient and quietest. Electricity use is set by the room's heat leak, not the label size, so the 2 ton costs about the same to run. A 1.5 ton is defensible only for a shaded ground-floor hall with closed doors, a 9 to 10 ft ceiling and one to three occupants. Price gap on the Gree Airy Plus is roughly Rs 25,000 to 35,000.

## Corrections applied after an adversarial verification pass (21 September 2026)

Four independent reviewers cross-checked the comparison page against the raw captures and the report, and each finding was re-checked by a second agent trying to refute it. 31 of 38 findings survived and were fixed in the page:

- Umar's Samsung link had an extra letter in the slug and returned 404; corrected.
- Gree Airy noise figures (19 dBA) are silent-mode minimums, not high-setting values. They are now shown as "n/s" with the dealer figure in brackets, so no Gree unit passes the 47 dB rule or sorts as quietest. Samsung's 44/28 remains the quietest published high-setting figure.
- The copper classifier missed Orient's "100% pure copper" wording and treated Midea's "100% copper connecting pipe" as a coil claim; both fixed. LG's "Cu/Al" from LG's own sheet is now labelled as a maker statement rather than a seller claim.
- The purification classifier matched "dehumidification" and "UV purification: No" as purification; fixed with word-bounded, negation-aware terms.
- Haier HSU-24HFCD, sold as the "without WiFi" SKU, no longer passes the WiFi filter; it is labelled "WiFi on a different SKU".
- Kenwood eLuxury Pro now reads EER 3.6 (Kenwood prints 3.4/3.6); PEL units with two conflicting EER figures on one page are treated as EER not stated.
- The shop's verbal quotes no longer count toward "best price".
- Two Gree offers sourced from friendshome.pk are now labelled "Friends Corporation (friendshome.pk)" rather than Friends Electronics; Friends' 24X Mega and EcoStar 24DU01WC-PRM are separate entries rather than merged into other stores' models.
- Kenwood's in-stock Luxury and Glory 2-ton lines (Kenwood direct only) were added alongside the E-Smart Onyx.
- Silence on a listing is now "n/s" rather than "No" for voice, WiFi and T3, except where a page states the absence.
- Spec values that came from a sibling model, a dealer sheet or the maker rather than the store page now carry that attribution.
- Changing a filter no longer leaves the dropdown showing a different model from the one in the comparison; sortable headers are keyboard operable with aria-sort; row buttons name the model; light-theme amber and red now meet 4.5:1 contrast.

Where the page and the report differ, it is deliberate: the page shows derived EERs for Gree units (marked "calc", from Gree or dealer wattage sheets) that the report's stricter "published by the store or maker" bar excludes.
