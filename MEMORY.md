# Session memory: 2-ton AC purchase research

Written 2026-09-21 (PKT). This file is the durable record of what was established in the research sessions of 20 and 21 September 2026 so that a future session can resume without re-deriving anything.

## Who and why

- Buyer: Fahd (GitHub fahdi), based in the Rawalpindi/Islamabad region.
- Purpose: one 2.0 ton wall-mounted DC inverter AC for a hall measuring 7 x 4 m (about 300 sq ft).
- Requirements in priority order: wall split only; WiFi app control (voice preferred); T3 tropical rating; highest EER, 4.0 preferred; low indoor noise (raised to a firm priority mid-session); copper condenser only if owner-confirmed, marketing claims do not count; air purification is a bonus; brand does not matter.
- Two verbal shop quotes to verify: "KES 24522" at Rs 300,000 and "KES 24625" at Rs 200,000.

## Settled facts

- "KES 24522" = Kenwood KES-2452FHI Superia Smart, a floor-standing cabinet (Japan Electronics, Umar and Kenwood all classify it so). Japan Electronics lists it at exactly Rs 300,000 (out of stock), Kenwood official Rs 365,000 (out of stock), Umar price on call. Excluded on requirement 1.
- "KES 24625" = Kenwood KES-2462S eSupreme Pro wall split. Umar Rs 220,000 in stock, so the shop quote is Rs 20,000 under web price. No WiFi, no T3, no EER on any listing; 60% saving, R32, gold fins. Absent from Kenwood's current official catalog. Fails requirements 2 and 3.
- The "known finding" that Japan Electronics lists neither quoted model was wrong: it lists the KES-2452 FHI. It does not list KES-2462S. It lists KES-2473S eSerene at Rs 220,000 (out of stock), and Kenwood's own eSerene page shows no WiFi, no voice, no T3.
- No 2-ton wall unit on sale at the three stores publishes EER 4.0. Best published with WiFi and T3: 3.6 (Midea Xtreme, Midea Breezeless E, Kenwood eLuxury Pro). Derived from maker or dealer wattage sheets: about 3.7 for Gree Airy Pro and Pular T3 15W, about 3.55 for Gree Airy Plus. Kenwood E-Smart Onyx KES-2466S has an official EER 4.0 with WiFi and T3 but is Kenwood-direct only and out of stock.
- No owner or reviewer confirmed a copper condenser on any model. Every copper mention is a seller claim. Midea's "100% copper pipe" refers to connecting pipe, not the coil.
- Noise: only 10 of 106 listings publish indoor dB. Quietest published high-setting figures: Samsung AR24ASFZGWKY 44/28, LG I24CGH 46 to 28 (dealer) or 56 to 32 (LG UAE sheet), Bosch 48.5/41/34, Haier HFPCA 47/43/39, TCL T3 Pro and T5 47. Gree Airy "19 dBA" is a silent-mode minimum and is treated as not stated. Haier HSU-24HFCD is loud at 62.
- Brand satisfaction in Pakistan (BrandReview.pk, Sept 2026): Gree 3.8 (292 reviews, 70% recommend), Panasonic 3.7 (22), Kenwood 3.6 (93), EcoStar 3.5, TCL 3.4 (service 2.0), Midea 3.4 (distributor churn), PEL 3.4, Samsung 3.4 (15), Haier 3.3 (build 2.0), Dawlance 3.0, Orient 2.9. After-sales is the universal complaint.
- Sizing: 2 ton is correct for the hall. Peak load about 6 to 6.5 kW (20,000 to 22,000 BTU/h) at 45C outside; a 1.5 ton derates to about 15,000 to 16,000 BTU/h and would run flat out; a 2 ton at 60 to 80 percent load is more efficient and quieter, and electricity use follows the room's heat leak rather than label size.

## Decisions

- Recommended purchase: Gree Airy Plus GS-24AITH21B-T3 at Rs 264,900 from Japan Electronics (Gul Noor Market, Murree Road, Rawalpindi, or Blue Area, Islamabad). In stock, WiFi, T3 G-Boost 68C, about EER 3.55, 10-year compressor and 5-year parts and service, Gree's owner rating. Inspect the outdoor coil for copper before paying; insist on vacuum-pump installation.
- Upgrade option: Gree Airy Pro GS-24AITH24W-T3 (adds voice control, UVC purification, SEER 15.8, about EER 3.7). Rs 294,900 from DWP Home (Gree's distributor, online, 042-111-111-397); Hanif Centre Lahore lists Rs 242,000 call-to-confirm; Electromart Rs 295,000; New Aftab Lahore Rs 289,900. Not stocked by the three local stores in 2 ton.
- Do not buy the quoted KES-2462S or the floor-standing KES-2452FHI.
- Runners-up if Gree is unavailable: Midea Breezeless E MSCB1DU-24HRFN8 (Friends Rs 216,900, Umar Rs 225,000; EER 3.6, built-in WiFi, R32, UV Air Magic+, weak Midea after-sales); TCL T5 Smart TAC-24T5-SMT-S (Rs 213,000 JE and Friends; EER 3.5, 47 dB, WiFi, T3, slow TCL service); Kenwood eLuxury Pro KEL-2470S (Kenwood direct Rs 273,600; voice, EER 3.6, 75% claim).
